Driver Help & Operational Guide

1. What is Multi-Platform Mission Chaining?

Vehicle transport freelancers often deliver a vehicle from City A to City B, but returning empty incurs high train/bus costs or unpaid travel time. This app solves that by finding and chaining complementary delivery missions:

  • Outbound Leg: Deliver a car from Barcelona to Tarragona with Otoqi.
  • Intermediate Leg: Pick up a relocation car in Tarragona and deliver to Zaragoza with Driiveme.
  • Return Leg: Take an assigned transfer in Zaragoza back to Barcelona with Onlogist.

2. Platform Checklist & Operational Handover

1 Otoqi Protocol
  • Open Otoqi Driver App before vehicle inspection.
  • Take min. 4 photos of exterior damages + odometer & fuel/EV %
  • Verify charging cable / tire kit present.
  • Digital delivery signature upon arrival.
2 Driiveme Protocol
  • Open Driiveme Pro Jockey order in app.
  • Validate inventory check-in & electronic CMR document.
  • Adhere to assigned delivery time slot (dealership / rental station).
  • Hand over keys and upload delivery confirmation.
3 Onlogist Protocol
  • Check order ID on Onlogist mobile portal.
  • Perform digital condition protocol (DCP) with client.
  • Retain toll / fuel receipts if reimbursable under order terms.
  • Sign handover protocol on recipient device.

3. Mobile Navigation & Deep Links

Every leg in the itinerary provides one-tap navigation:

  • Google Maps: Launches the Google Maps app with origin and destination pre-loaded.
  • Waze: Launches Waze navigation with live traffic and speed camera warnings.
  • Platform App: Directly opens the corresponding driver portal or native app on your phone.

4. Security & Credentials Privacy

All credentials entered in Settings are kept exclusively inside your browser's local storage (client-side only). No keys, passwords, or personal tokens are ever transmitted to any third-party server or central database.
